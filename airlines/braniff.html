Braniff International in 1979

Ghostflights has 3,908 of Braniff International's flights a week from the Official Airline Guide, North American Edition, November 15, 1979. The busiest airports in that schedule are Dallas, Kansas City and Oklahoma City.

Braniff was gone by 1985, so on Ghostflights it flies its 1979 schedule.
