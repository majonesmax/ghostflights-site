Ghostflights feed

The flights on Ghostflights as data. Departed airlines fly their real 1985 schedules and this is where they are right now.

Not real traffic

  • Every record says "simulated": true and "year": 1985.
  • There's no ICAO address on anything. Nothing here is shaped to slip into a real tracker.
  • Flight numbers come only from a printed timetable. The feed never makes one up.
  • Only airlines that are gone. No airline flying today appears, not even in its old paint.

Use it

It runs in the page with no server. Two files hold everything: feed.js, the schedule engine, and feed-data.js, the schedules. The flights come from the clock, so every copy agrees.

<script src="feed-data.js"></script>
<script src="feed.js"></script>
<script>
  const flights = Ghostflights.feed();          // right now
  const later = Ghostflights.feed(Date.now() + 3600e3);  // in an hour
</script>

Or in Node:

require('./feed-data.js');
require('./feed.js');
console.log(globalThis.Ghostflights.feed());

Where it comes from


    
  
  • Northwest Orient: the system timetable of October 27, 1985, from the Northwest Airlines History Center.
    • 
  
  • Republic: city schedules from 1984 to 1986, from the Northwest Airlines History Center.
    • 
  
  • Western: the Official Airline Guide of February 15, 1985, as transcribed at departedflights.com.
    • 



Each flight runs on the weekday it flew in 1985 for today's date. Positions follow the great circle between the two airports. Altitude and speed are estimates from the distance and the scheduled time.
